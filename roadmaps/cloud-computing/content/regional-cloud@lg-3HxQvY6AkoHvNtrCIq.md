# Regional Cloud

Regional cloud providers operate in specific geographies and are often chosen for data residency, latency, or cost reasons. Examples include Hetzner and OVH in Europe, and UpCloud in the Nordics. They typically offer core IaaS services at competitive prices but with a smaller service catalog and fewer managed offerings than hyperscalers.
