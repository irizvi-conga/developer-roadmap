# Upstream Connectivity
 
Upstream connectivity is how a data center connects to the public internet. Providers maintain multiple redundant fiber connections to upstream ISPs and peer directly with other networks at internet exchange points (IXPs) to reduce latency and transit costs. Redundant upstream links ensure that a single cable cut or ISP outage does not take a data center offline.
