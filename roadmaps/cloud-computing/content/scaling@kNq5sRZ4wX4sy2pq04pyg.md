# Scaling
 
Scaling is the process of adjusting compute capacity to match demand. Cloud environments support both automatic scaling in response to real-time load and pricing-based strategies that trade reliability guarantees for lower cost. Getting scaling right reduces both over-provisioning (wasted spend) and under-provisioning (degraded performance or outages).
