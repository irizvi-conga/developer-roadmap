# Developer-Focused

Developer-focused cloud providers simplify deployment and operations for smaller teams and individual developers. DigitalOcean, Render, and Fly fall into this category. They offer a streamlined experience with fewer services but lower operational complexity, clear pricing, and good developer tooling. Often the right choice for startups, side projects, and teams without dedicated infrastructure engineers.
