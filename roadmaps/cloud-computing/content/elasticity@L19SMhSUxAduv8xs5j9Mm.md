# Elasticity

Elasticity is the ability of a system to automatically provision and release resources in response to demand. A highly elastic system scales out when traffic increases and scales back in when it drops, paying only for what it uses. Elasticity is one of the defining properties of cloud computing and the basis for auto scaling and serverless architectures.
