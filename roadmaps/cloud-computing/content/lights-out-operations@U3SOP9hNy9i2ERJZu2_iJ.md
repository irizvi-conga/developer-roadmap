# Lights-Out Operations

A lights-out data center is designed to operate without on-site staff. Hardware is monitored, managed, and in many cases repaired remotely through out-of-band management interfaces like IPMI or iDRAC. Cloud providers operate lights-out facilities at scale, reducing labor costs and enabling faster incident response through automation rather than physical intervention.
