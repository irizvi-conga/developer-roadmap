# Data Protection
 
Data protection covers the controls that keep sensitive data secure at rest, in transit, and throughout its lifecycle. In cloud environments, this includes encryption, secrets management, and compliance with privacy regulations. These controls apply across storage, databases, APIs, and any service that handles customer or business data.
