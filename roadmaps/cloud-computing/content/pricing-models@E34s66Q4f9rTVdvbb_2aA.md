# Pricing Models

Cloud providers offer several pricing models that trade cost for flexibility and commitment. On-demand pricing charges by the second or hour with no commitment. Reserved and committed-use pricing offers significant discounts in exchange for a term commitment. Spot and preemptible pricing uses spare capacity at steep discounts but with the risk of interruption. Understanding these models is necessary for making informed infrastructure decisions.
