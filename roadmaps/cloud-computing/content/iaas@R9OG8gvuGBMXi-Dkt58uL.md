# IaaS
 
IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service) gives you raw computing resources over the internet: virtual machines, storage, and networking, without the overhead of managing physical hardware. You control the operating system and everything above it; the provider manages the physical layer. EC2, Google Compute Engine, and Azure Virtual Machines are IaaS products. IaaS is the foundation most other cloud service models build on.
