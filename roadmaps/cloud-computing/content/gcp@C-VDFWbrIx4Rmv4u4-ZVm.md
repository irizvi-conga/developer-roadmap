# GCP
 
GCP (Google Cloud Platform) is Google's public cloud, known for its strengths in data analytics, machine learning, and Kubernetes, which Google originally developed. BigQuery, Vertex AI, and Google Kubernetes Engine are among its most widely used services. GCP has a smaller market share than AWS and Azure but is particularly strong in data-intensive and AI-driven workloads.
