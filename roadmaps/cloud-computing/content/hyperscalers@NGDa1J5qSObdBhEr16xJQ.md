# Hyperscalers

Hyperscalers are cloud providers that operate at massive global scale, with data centers in dozens of regions and hundreds of services covering compute, storage, networking, AI, databases, and more. AWS, Google Cloud Platform, and Microsoft Azure are the three hyperscalers. They set the pace for cloud innovation and account for the majority of global cloud market share.
