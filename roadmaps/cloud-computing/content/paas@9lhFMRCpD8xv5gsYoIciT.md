# PaaS
 
PaaS (Platform as a Service) provides a managed environment for deploying and running applications, abstracting away the underlying infrastructure. You bring your code; the platform handles the runtime, scaling, patching, and availability. Google App Engine, AWS Elastic Beanstalk, and Heroku are PaaS examples. PaaS trades infrastructure control for reduced operational burden.
