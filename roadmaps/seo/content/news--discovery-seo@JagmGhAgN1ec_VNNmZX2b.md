# News & Discovery SEO

News and Discovery SEO is about earning visibility in Google surfaces that recommend content without a typed query, such as Top Stories, Google News, and the Discover feed. These surfaces favor fresh, original, well-sourced content with clear authorship and publish dates, high-quality images, and headlines that describe the story accurately rather than relying on clickbait.
