# Benchmarking and Profiling

Benchmarking and profiling are how inference engineers turn intuition into evidence. Benchmarking measures system-level throughput and latency under load; profiling zooms in to identify which specific operations are consuming time. Both are prerequisites for making optimization decisions with confidence.
