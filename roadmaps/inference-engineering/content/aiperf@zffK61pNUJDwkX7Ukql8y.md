# AIPerf

AIPerf is an open-source benchmarking framework designed to evaluate the performance of artificial intelligence hardware and software stacks by measuring their ability to execute large-scale neural network models. It simulates real-world workloads to provide standardized metrics, such as throughput and latency, allowing developers to compare how different systems handle complex computational tasks.
