# Testing and Deployment

Deploying inference services safely requires tools for rolling out changes without downtime, estimating costs before committing to a configuration, and maintaining visibility into system health once live. This group covers the deployment, cost, observability, and compliance concerns that apply once a service is ready for production traffic.
